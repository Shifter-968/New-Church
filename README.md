# AfriChurch — fresh React edition

An independent React **JavaScript** application, with a new Supabase database and Netlify deployment configuration. It does not connect to, migrate, or modify the previous system.

## What works in this first slice

- A public landing page and debounced church search backed by Supabase RPCs, including branch selection for individual registration.
- Church registration creates an email/password or Google-authenticated account and a pending application. Active plans and their published prices are loaded from the plan catalogue; an active plan must be selected, and paid plans require a payment method and transaction reference. The app does not collect payments. After email confirmation (Google accounts are already verified), the applicant can sign in and optionally upload one PDF, JPEG or PNG receipt (up to 8 MB), then explicitly submit the details, with or without a receipt. A platform owner must independently verify payment and approve or reject the application; approval creates the church, its organization administrator assignment and the requested plan assignment. If the requested plan has since been deactivated, approval is blocked until it is active again. Changes to plan features affect the assigned plan, while the application's displayed price is a registration-time snapshot. Existing pre-migration applications without a selected plan retain their prior approval flow.
- Registration receipts are stored in the private `registration-payment-proofs` Supabase Storage bucket. A confirmed applicant may upload or remove their receipt only before submitting; the applicant and platform owners can download a submitted receipt. A receipt or reference does not prove payment. MIME and size restrictions do not validate file contents: treat downloaded files as untrusted. There is no automatic malware scanning, retention or cleanup policy; establish these before accepting real payment documents. Once submitted, corrections require contacting the platform owner; no correction interface is implemented. Plan and payment details are stored with the application; do not enter bank credentials or other sensitive information.
- Supabase email/password sign-in and sign-out. The sign-in screen can request a password-recovery email; the link opens a new-password form. A recovery session is required, mismatched passwords are rejected, and Supabase enforces its configured password policy. The recovery link must be opened in the browser in which the user intends to reset the password.
- Google sign-in is available on the sign-in and registration forms. Google registration creates a church application or individual registration using the verified Google email; the email entered in the form must match the Google account. Church registration through Google requires applying [supabase/035_google_organization_registration.sql](supabase/035_google_organization_registration.sql). Existing accounts with the same verified email may be linked by Supabase. Registration details are held temporarily in browser session storage during OAuth and cleared after completion or when discarded.
- Platform owners can create and select church workspaces.
- Platform owners can create and edit the plan catalogue, including pricing, limits and feature flags, and manually assign a plan to a church. Approval of a new application assigns its requested plan. Billing displays trial, due and grace-period dates and current member, visitor, branch and account usage. The platform owner manually records a verified payment and reference; AfriChurch does not collect payment or automatically verify it. Usage limits are informational and do not block activity.
- A member directory with search (including both phone numbers), branch/status filters, add, edit and removal, subject to database-enforced permissions and organization/branch scope. Filtered CSV export includes accessible records and protects spreadsheet formula-like cells by prefixing an apostrophe. Import is CSV-only: download the template, enter up to 1,500 new members (2 MB maximum), and choose one branch where you can both view and create members. The template uses `first_name,last_name,gender,phone,phone_2,date_of_birth,residence,baptised,marital_status`. Common header variants (such as `surname`, `sex`, `DOB`, and `baptized`) are mapped automatically; generated IDs, branch IDs and timestamps are ignored. First name, last name, gender and date of birth are required; marital status defaults to `SINGLE` when absent, and the supported values are Single, Courtship, Engaged, Married, Divorced and Widowed. Invalid required values identify the CSV row. Email and other optional contact details may be omitted. Possible matches by full name, email or either digit-normalized phone are checked in batches against visible members and within the file; potential matches block the import until reviewed. Matching does not prove identity and cannot prevent concurrent additions. Manual creation and editing require the same core profile fields. Imported records are inserted, not updates; check the directory before retrying a request with an uncertain connection. The export includes a branch column for reporting and is not directly an import template.
- Individual email/password or Google registration for members and visitors. After email confirmation (Google accounts are already verified) and sign-in, a member is linked automatically only when exactly one active record in the selected church and branch has matching first and last names and at least one matching stored and submitted phone number (punctuation is ignored). The matching record must also be unlinked and not reserved for another approved registration. Email and birth date do not determine the match. **A name and phone number are not proof of identity or phone ownership**: publicly known, shared or guessed numbers can allow impersonation. For stronger protection, use independent identity verification or a verified-phone challenge before public use.
- Missing or mismatched phone numbers, mismatched names, multiple matches and unavailable records create a Membership request, even when no suggested record is found. Users with `members.update` permission in the branch can review suggested matches; searching for and approving a different active, unlinked record in the same branch also requires `members.view`. A reviewer must independently verify identity before approving or rejecting. The applicant must check status after approval to complete registration. Existing pending reviews retain their prior status. Registration creates a basic role with no management permissions and a private profile. Branch, status and deletion are temporarily blocked for linked member records until account-access management can safely synchronize access. Existing accounts cannot yet register as an individual through this flow, and visitors do not yet have a full visitor dashboard.
- Visitor management includes scoped viewing, adding, editing and deleting unlinked records, search, branch filters and CSV import/export. Self-registered visitors appear in this directory; their branch cannot be changed and their records cannot be deleted until account access management can synchronize their access. Directory contact changes do not update account profiles. Email and phone are optional for manually added visitors. Manual creation warns about up to five potential matches across records the user can view. CSV import (up to 1,500 rows or 2 MB into one permitted branch) checks potential matches in batches; it adds records without updating them. Search and export cover at most 1,000 loaded accessible visitors. Follow-ups, conversion to member, visitor status and the previous system's additional visitor fields are not yet available.
- Visitor self-registration checks the selected church and branch for a matching full name, confirmed account email or either submitted phone. Possible matches, including already linked records, require review in Visitors by someone with `visitors.update` permission for that branch. A reviewer must independently verify identity before linking an available record; they may instead approve a new record for a different person or reject the request. After approval, the visitor signs in and checks status to complete registration; linking retains the existing visitor ID and attendance history. A changed account email or unavailable approved record blocks completion and requires contacting the church. No possible match uses the existing new-visitor registration flow. Matching is not a uniqueness guarantee: other branches, existing duplicates and concurrent manual registration are not reconciled, and previously created duplicate records are not merged. Review decisions are not currently in the audit trail.
- An overview based on all accessible members and branches. Directory, search, filters and exports use paginated member loading.
- Organization-wide or branch-scoped assignments, and permission bundles stored in the database.
- **Organization settings** lets organization administrators and platform owners rename their church, control public discoverability, and independently allow member and visitor self-registration. Hiding a church removes it from public search and pauses incomplete individual registrations; disabling a type likewise pauses pending and approved-but-not-completed registrations until re-enabled. Existing registered accounts, administrator-created records and organization applications are unaffected. The signup form checks current settings before account creation, and the database enforces them again at completion; settings can change between those steps, so an account may still be created but unable to complete until registration reopens. Settings changes are audited as metadata, without before/after values. Only organization-wide administrators and platform owners can manage these settings. Branch locations remain under Branches; branding, timezone, currency and other settings are not implemented.
- Permission-aware workspace navigation and branch creation/branch administrator assignment for organization administrators and owners. An administrator can assign an existing confirmed Supabase Auth account by email; account invitations are not yet implemented. Migration 014 grants the standard branch administrator member, visitor and attendance permissions in its branch. Only roles matching the previous built-in permission bundle are automatically upgraded; customized conflicting roles require administrator review.
- An account can have only one active church/branch assignment. A confirmed email already assigned to another church or branch is rejected with a clear database error; use a different account email for a separate assignment.
- Organization administrators and platform owners can set each branch's coordinates, label and attendance radius (20–2,000 metres) under Branches. Search a city/address on the OpenStreetMap map and click the church location, or use device location after browser permission. Map tiles and place search require internet access; the public Nominatim geocoder is rate-limited and intended for occasional manual searches, not bulk geocoding.
- Members and visitors with a linked record and active branch assignment can use **My attendance** to check in to an ongoing church event. The server verifies the event time, assignment and distance from their own configured branch; device location requires browser permission and HTTPS (or localhost). GPS coordinates reported by a browser can be inaccurate or spoofed and are not proof of physical presence. Coordinates are used for the check but not stored in attendance. The existing calendar events are church-wide, not branch-specific: each person checks in against their own branch location, not the event's text location. Multiple ongoing events require choosing the intended one. A branch without configured coordinates cannot self-check in.
- **Attendance** allows authorized administrators to search existing members or visitors and mark them present for an event, including past events, without GPS. This creates the same record as self-check-in. Attendance can be filtered by person type, branch and name, and exported as CSV. Branch scope and `attendance.view`/`attendance.create` and corresponding directory view permissions are enforced in the database; organization administrators and platform owners have access. There can be only one active check-in per person per event. Members and visitors with one unambiguous linked record and an active matching assignment can see their latest 100 visible check-ins in My attendance; records for events they no longer have permission to see are hidden. Organization administrators and platform owners can remove an incorrect check-in with a required reason. The person is no longer counted present, can check in again, and the original record, actor and reason remain in a correction history visible to those administrators. The latest 100 corrections per event are displayed; reasons should not include unnecessary sensitive details. Events and people with active or corrected attendance cannot be deleted, preserving records. Editing timestamps, statuses other than present, per-event branches and full attendee pagination are not implemented. Lists are limited to 100 events, 1,000 attendance records per event and 1,000 accessible people of each type.
- **Reported absences:** members and visitors with one linked record and a matching active branch assignment can report or update an optional reason for visible upcoming or ongoing events from My attendance. They can withdraw visible notices, including for past events; the list is limited to 100 upcoming events and past notices combined, with upcoming events first. A notice is separate from an attendance record and does not count someone as absent in attendance or reports. Someone already marked present cannot report an absence; if they are marked present later, the notice remains and is shown as present until withdrawn. Organization administrators, platform owners, and users specifically granted `attendance.absences.view` can view notices, names, phone numbers and reasons within their assignment's branch scope, including for targeted events they may not otherwise be able to view. Branch administrators are not automatically granted this permission; an organization administrator can assign a custom role with it. Search, filters and CSV export cover the latest 1,000 accessible notices only. Reasons are optional and should not contain unnecessary sensitive information. Notices restrict deletion of their event or linked person until withdrawn. No automatic notifications, leader-specific routing, acknowledgement workflow, or automatic absence detection are implemented.
- **Events** provides create/edit/delete and audience controls from a management navigation entry, plus a link to the Attendance workspace for attendee search, filters and export. The calendar has month, week timetable and day views and loads up to 1,000 events in the visible date range. New events can repeat daily, weekly on selected weekdays, or monthly at a chosen interval through an end date; each occurrence is a separate event and edits/deletes affect only that occurrence. Event locations accept free text, with an optional OpenStreetMap pin and place search for in-person events. The pin is informational and does not change attendance's branch-radius check. Apply migration 040 before using recurrence or map pins. The owner calendar can filter across churches; attendance uses the church selected in the workspace.
- **Reports & analytics** is available to organization administrators and platform owners for the selected church. The platform owner assigns an active plan; the database independently checks reporting inclusions on every report request. No assigned active plan means neither analytics nor advanced reporting is available. Analytics provides total active members, visitors, branches and active check-in records in the last 30 days; advanced reporting provides counts per branch and a CSV export. Billing dates and usage are informational; expired/grace status does not automatically disable access, and plan usage limits are not enforced.
- **Families and Children** maintain branch-scoped family names, contact, address and notes, and separate child records with names, optional date of birth and residence, and parent/guardian contact. Link existing members and children to families in the same branch and record their relationship; a person can belong to multiple families. Families use member permissions. Child records support the separate children permissions described below, while existing member permissions continue to work for children; linking and unlinking require member update permission. Only collect necessary information, as authorized users can see child and family details in their permitted branch. Family, child and link lists each load up to 1,000 records, and person search shows at most 30 matches from loaded records. Remove family links before moving a linked member to a different branch or deleting a linked child. Family and child branches cannot currently be changed; family deletion removes its links, not the people. Children are separate records and do not automatically become members or accounts.
- Organization administrators and platform owners can create, rename and delete ministries and departments and add or remove members and visitors. Teams are church-wide and can include people from different branches. Ministry criteria can combine gender, age range, marital status, baptism and residence; all selected criteria must match, and qualifying active members are assigned automatically when their profile or the ministry criteria is saved. Automatic assignments are reconciled without deleting manual assignments; automatically assigned members cannot be manually removed while they still match. Departments and visitors remain manual. Lists are paginated across all accessible records. Only linked, actively assigned accounts receive a team's targeted content. Team setup currently requires organization administrator or platform owner access.
- Account profile editing, password change, personal notification inbox, announcements and a church event calendar. An event or announcement can target everyone or any combination of existing roles, ministries and departments; anyone matching **any** selected audience sees it. Organization administrators and platform owners can always see and manage targeted content for support, even if not selected. Branch administrators see targeted content only when their role or team is selected. Audience membership is checked dynamically using active role assignments and linked member/visitor records; revocation removes access. Role audiences match the role across the church, including branch-scoped assignments, because events are not branch-specific. Attendance self-check-in and event/attendance lists enforce the event audience; a permitted administrator may manually mark a person outside the audience. Existing records remain visible to everyone after migration 015 until explicitly changed. Teams and roles used in an audience cannot be deleted until audiences are updated. Platform owners can filter and manage records across churches, and organization administrators can manage their own church. Profiles are private account records, not synchronized to member records. The calendar has month, week timetable and day views, defaults to ongoing and upcoming events, and loads up to 1,000 records in the visible range.
- **Church updates** in Notifications shows the latest 100 currently visible notices across churches for announcements and events created after migration 028. Each user can mark a notice or all shown notices read and refresh the inbox; this is separate from personal notifications. Visibility is evaluated against current audiences and assignments, so joining later can reveal an existing notice and revoking access hides it. Edits update the displayed title without creating a new notice; deleting the source deletes its notice and read markers. Existing announcements and events are not backfilled. There are no automatic reminders, absence notifications, push notifications or email delivery.
- Organization administrators and platform owners can create, edit, delete and assign **custom roles** to confirmed accounts already actively assigned to their church. Choose an explicit branch or entire-church scope; permissions add to existing roles rather than replacing them. Accounts may hold multiple active role assignments within one church, but not active assignments in multiple churches. The editor supports member, visitor and children view/create/update/delete, attendance view/create and reported-absence viewing; other permissions and user invitations are not yet implemented. Attendance marking also requires the appropriate member/visitor directory view permission. Empty roles work for audiences. Built-in roles and roles created outside this editor cannot be modified here. Remove assignments and audience references before deleting a custom role. At most 1,000 active custom assignments are shown. Role edits affect all its assignees immediately in database checks; users should reload to refresh dashboard navigation.
- **Audit trail** shows organization administrators their own church's recorded activity; platform owners can filter across churches. Each record contains the action, timestamp, actor ID and resource ID, not before/after values, personal details or message content. Announcements/events and Connect activity were captured by earlier migrations; migration 018 starts recording member, visitor, branch, role, assignment, ministry/department, team membership and attendance changes, and migration 020 adds family, child and link changes from that point onward, with no backfill. Registration requests, plan changes and other modules are not fully audited. Only the latest 100 records are loaded and searched; this is not a complete audit trail or a tamper-proof compliance log.
- **Users & access** lets organization administrators and platform owners view and search the first 1,000 active assignments in a selected church. Custom and branch-administrator assignments can be removed only when another active assignment remains; organization administrators and other built-in roles are protected. Removal affects only that assignment, not the account or other roles, and is recorded in the audit trail. Users should reload their workspace after changes. Branch administrators and other non-organization-admin users cannot use this screen, even if an existing role exposes the navigation item. Invitations, complete account removal, and synchronization with member or visitor records are not yet implemented.
- **Children** has separate `children.view`, `children.create`, `children.update` and `children.delete` custom-role permissions. Existing member permissions continue to provide backward-compatible access. Children’s ministry teachers can be assigned a custom role with child-only scope without gaining member, family or visitor access. Authorized users can manage child records, import CSV files matching the child form, create branch-specific children’s events, manually record attendance while an event is ongoing, and correct attendance later. The CSV import ignores record, organization and branch IDs; choose the destination branch in the app. No child self check-in is available.
- Community includes Connect groups: platform owners and organization administrators can manage group setup across their church scope without joining, and assign a confirmed, actively assigned church account as owner. An assigned church user can create a group, and eligible users can join an active group by code. A branch-specific group requires an organization-wide or matching branch assignment. Creators can add, remove, ban, restore and promote/demote members; church administrators can assist with membership, while only creators can change group admin roles. Members and group admins can leave even when the group is archived or suspended; they immediately lose access to its conversation and can rejoin an active group with its code if eligible. Rejoining restores ordinary membership, not group admin privileges; previous messages remain. A creator must have a church administrator transfer ownership before leaving. Leaving is recorded as a membership update in support activity. Active group members can send and edit their own text messages and delete their own messages for everyone; creators and group admins can delete any group message for everyone. Deletion replaces the message content with a tombstone; it is not a guarantee of erasure from backups. Non-member support cannot read or delete messages. Group, membership and message changes are included as metadata in support activity, not message content. Lists show at most 100 groups and the latest 100 messages; use Refresh to see updates. Media, granular roles, invitations and direct conversations are not yet available.
- **Connect announcements** are a separate board inside each active group. Group creators and admins can create and edit drafts, publish or archive announcements, choose normal/important/urgent priority, pin items, and permanently delete them. Drafts and archived items are visible only to group creators and admins; published items are visible to all active group members with an eligible church assignment. Switching a published item to draft or archived hides it from other members; publishing it again makes it visible again. Priority is a visual label, not a notification. Church administrators and platform support cannot access the content unless they are active members. Losing membership or assignment, or archiving/suspending the group, removes access; existing content becomes visible again if access is restored. Deleting a group deletes its announcements. Up to 100 accessible items are displayed, ordered pinned first, then priority, then creation time; a leader's drafts and archives share that limit with published items. Each member can mark an item read privately. Title or message edits and republication make it unread again; changing only priority or pin does not. Read markers survive leaving and rejoining and are deleted with the announcement. Existing announcements start unread. No read-by list or delivery guarantee is provided. Use manual refresh to see changes. Announcement changes are recorded as metadata in support activity, not content. Existing announcements remain published after migration 031. Scheduled publishing and automatic notifications are not implemented.

Audience options are subject to the Supabase API's configured row limit. Do not delete a role or team while another administrator is simultaneously adding it to an audience: the deletion check is not concurrency-safe and a stale selection could remain, preventing a later edit until the audience is corrected. Stale identifiers do not grant access.

Online payment collection and automatic verification, advanced event-series management, advanced attendance correction, advanced team management, visitor follow-ups, comprehensive reporting, full role and user administration, full community functionality, full member/visitor self-service dashboards and the rest of the previous application's functionality are **not implemented yet**. Navigation placeholders are not functional modules. Payment is arranged outside the app and manually verified by the platform owner. This is a foundation, not a feature-complete replacement.

The SQL tests in `tests/` are intended only for an isolated, disposable PostgreSQL cluster. On a fresh cluster, run `tests/connect_test_auth.sql`, `tests/storage_test_standin.sql`, `supabase/schema.sql`, migrations 002 through 010 in order, `tests/individual_registration.sql`, `tests/visitor_migration_before.sql`, migration 011, `tests/visitor_migration_after.sql`, migrations 012 and 013, `tests/attendance_migration_before.sql`, migration 014, `tests/attendance_migration_after.sql`, and migrations 015 through 043 in order. Then run the Connect, member registration name/phone, visitor management, visitor duplicate/location, event attendance, audience, custom roles, personal attendance history, attendance corrections, church audit, families/children, children events, plan reporting, users/access, visitor registration review, reported absence, organization settings, member duplicate, church update notification, leave Connect, Connect announcement, Connect announcement workflow, Connect announcement reads and registration payment proof tests with `psql -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1`. The event attendance test covers physical and virtual self-check-in plus administrator time-window enforcement and must run after migration 042. The children events test verifies child-only role scope and manual, branch-matched event attendance and must run after migration 043. The member registration name/phone test verifies matching without relying on first name and the missing-saved-phone contact message. The original individual registration test exercises pre-034 behavior and must run before migration 034. The Auth and Storage stand-ins are not Supabase and must never be applied to a live project. They do not test the real Storage API, MIME or size enforcement. A passing local test does not replace testing against real Supabase accounts and policies.

## 2. Run locally

Copy `.env.example` to `.env.local` and fill in the project's URL and **publishable or anon** key from Supabase. These values are public client configuration; access control is enforced by the SQL policies. In PowerShell:

```powershell
cd "New Church System"
npm install
npm run dev
```

Register a church with a new account, confirm its email, then sign in as the owner and open **Church applications** to approve it. The applicant can then sign in to their workspace. No example or old church data is loaded.

The React app connects through the Supabase API and does **not** need the PostgreSQL connection string or database password. Keep those credentials out of `.env.local`, Netlify's `VITE_` variables, and browser code. Use the Supabase SQL editor for the SQL setup above, or a trusted server-side database client if needed. The supplied connection URI ends in `/postgres`, which is the database name in that URI; verify the actual database name in Supabase before changing it.

### Google sign-in and registration

Follow [Supabase's Google sign-in setup](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/social-sign-in/auth-google). Create a Google OAuth **web application**, configure its consent screen, add the app's origin as an authorized JavaScript origin, and add the Supabase project's callback URL shown in the Google provider settings as an authorized redirect URI. Configure the client ID and secret in Supabase Auth's Google provider, not in frontend environment variables. Add each app origin's `/login` URL to Supabase's redirect allowlist. Apply migration 035 for Google-based church applications. Google sign-in and registration are enabled in the app without a feature flag; redeploy after code changes. ## 3. Set up branches and staff access

Organization administrators can create branches and assign a branch administrator by the email of an existing confirmed account in the Branches screen. Invitations and normal account creation for staff are not implemented. Until role management screens are implemented, a trusted administrator can use the SQL editor for additional assignments. Replace all placeholder UUIDs with real IDs; the staff user must already exist in Supabase Auth. The SQL editor bypasses row-level security, so access to it must be restricted.

```sql
-- Find the organization ID after the owner creates it in the app.
select id, name from public.organizations;

-- Optional branch.
insert into public.branches (organization_id, name)
values ('organization-uuid', 'Main branch');

-- Organization-wide administrator: all member actions in this organization.
insert into public.roles (organization_id, name, is_org_admin)
values ('organization-uuid', 'Organization Administrator', true)
returning id;

insert into public.assignments (user_id, organization_id, role_id)
values ('staff-auth-user-uuid', 'organization-uuid', 'role-uuid');

-- Example branch-scoped role, with explicit member permissions.
insert into public.roles (organization_id, name, permissions)
values ('organization-uuid', 'Branch Member Manager',
        array['members.view', 'members.create', 'members.update', 'members.delete'])
returning id;

insert into public.assignments (user_id, organization_id, branch_id, role_id)
values ('staff-auth-user-uuid', 'organization-uuid', 'branch-uuid', 'role-uuid');
```

An organization-wide assignment has a null `branch_id`. A branch-scoped assignment only covers members in that branch; members without a branch are accessible only to organization-wide assignments or platform owners. `is_org_admin` grants full member access only when the assignment is organization-wide. Other roles use `members.view`, `members.create`, `members.update`, and `members.delete`; include `members.view` for anyone managing records. Assignments may be disabled using `active = false`. Multiple assignments contribute their permissions within their individual scopes. Additional navigation permissions are listed in `src/navigation.js`, but their underlying modules and server-side policies are not yet implemented. Hiding navigation is not a security boundary. The previous app's other permission categories and scope types have not yet been implemented.

Churches are discoverable by default; set `organizations.is_discoverable` to false for a church that should not appear in public search. Search and branch lookup expose only IDs and names to unauthenticated visitors. Church-scoped sign-in selects the requested church only if the signed-in user has access; it does not grant access.

### Install AfriChurch on a device

The site includes a web app manifest, browser icons and an Apple touch icon.
